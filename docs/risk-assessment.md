# Risk Assessment

## Overview

Risk assessment helps identify, analyze, and prioritize cybersecurity and AI-related threats in autonomous vehicles. Understanding the likelihood and potential impact of each threat enables organizations to implement effective security controls.

## Risk Assessment Process

### 1. Identify Assets
Determine the critical assets that require protection, including:
- AI models
- Sensors (LiDAR, Radar, Cameras, GPS)
- Vehicle communication systems
- Control software
- Cloud services

### 2. Identify Threats
Common threats include:
- Sensor spoofing
- Adversarial AI attacks
- Malware
- Unauthorized access
- Data tampering
- Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks

### 3. Analyze Vulnerabilities
Evaluate weaknesses that attackers could exploit, such as:
- Weak authentication
- Unpatched software
- Insecure communication channels
- Poor access control
- Misconfigured systems

### 4. Evaluate Risk

| Threat | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level |
|---------|------------|--------|------------|
| Sensor Spoofing | High | High | Critical |
| Adversarial AI | Medium | High | High |
| Malware | Medium | High | High |
| Data Manipulation | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| DoS Attack | Low | High | Medium |

### 5. Prioritize Mitigation
Address high-risk threats first by implementing appropriate security controls and continuously reviewing the effectiveness of those controls.

## Conclusion

A structured risk assessment process helps improve the safety, reliability, and resilience of autonomous vehicle systems by identifying security risks before they can be exploited.
